# IMAGE_AND_CARDS_POLICY.md

## Media storage

- Do not store image binaries as BLOBs in MySQL.
- Store files in filesystem/hosting or external image storage if added later.
- DB stores metadata: URL/path, alt text, type, size, dimensions, owner entity.
- Videos are external URLs, never uploaded video files in MVP.

## Card/cromo policy

### First Team

First Team cards are premium and more elaborate, but they must be built in the web by layers/components.

Do not use a final complete PNG as the card.

The card should compose:

- player photo/base image;
- background/treatment layer;
- shirt number;
- public name;
- position;
- country flag/nationality;
- dominant foot indicator;
- goals;
- assists;
- relevant stats by type.

This allows stats and text to update without replacing the whole card image.

### Academy/rest teams

Academy cards are simpler and also generated by the web.

They show:

- photo or placeholder;
- public name;
- shirt number;
- position;
- country flag/nationality;
- dominant foot;
- goals;
- assists.

## Dominant foot

If reasonable, show two simple foot icons or labels:

- left foot;
- right foot.

Mark the dominant one active. If unknown, show both neutral or hide the indicator.

## Upload permissions

- Superadmin and manager can upload/manage images.
- Coaches cannot upload images, cards, logos or news media.
